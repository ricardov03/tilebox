# tilebox v0.2.0

## Summary
This version adds a Site tab where you set the page title, the description and the social preview image, and it builds your favicon and a social card for you. A link tile now fills in its own icon, title and description from the address you paste, and any tile can be hidden, duplicated, scheduled or given a spotlight. Your email address no longer appears anywhere in the published files, so address harvesters cannot read it. The editor also gains a photo search, a QR code, a share button, a contact card to download, and a red trash button to remove a tile.
